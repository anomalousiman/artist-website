IMAN HOWARD — WEBSITE

This is a clean four-page static website with a minimal white layout inspired by the reference artist site: spaced uppercase navigation, subtle grey dividers, and a centered artwork slideshow on Home.

FILES
- index.html: Home and the rotating artwork images
- exhibitions.html: exhibition history
- about.html: artist biography
- contact.html: contact details
- styles.css: all fonts, colours, spacing and layout
- script.js: image rotation and current-year footer

PERSONALISE IT
- Replace bracketed copy on Exhibitions and About with your own details.
- Update the email address and Instagram link in contact.html.
- Replace the image URLs in script.js with your own published image URLs. Keep the four entries to have four rotating images, or add/remove entries there.
- The image URLs currently point to sample artwork photography and require an internet connection. Use your own artwork images before publishing.
- The four page links are Home, Exhibitions, About and Contact.

Upload all files together to the top level of your GitHub repository. Keep the stylesheet named styles.css; index.html links to that exact filename.
