About Iman

An introduction to the artist, the ideas behind the work and the things that continue to inspire the practice.

Making space for stories.

Iman Howard is a visual artist whose practice explores identity, atmosphere and the expressive possibilities of colour and form.

Working across painting and drawing, Iman brings together observation and imagination to create work that invites viewers to pause, look closely and find their own point of connection.
